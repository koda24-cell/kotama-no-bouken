# Visual-first design engine — 2026-11-24
User correction: gaps, overlaps and intersections are permitted. This supersedes structural interference gates in earlier design-engine planning. Never reject merely for internal topology, non-manifold geometry or physical assembly infeasibility. Visible unintended defects remain review targets; intentional gaps and protrusions are allowed. Gameplay collision, export validity and performance remain separate existing requirements.
## Official research and applicability
- Open3D first-hit ray casting: https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/tutorial/geometry/ray_casting.html and https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/python_api/open3d.t.geometry.RaycastingScene.html . First-hit depth, normals and geometry IDs can isolate visible surfaces irrespective of hidden overlaps. Use fixed orthographic rays for front/back/left/right/top and oblique cameras; compare only matched camera/scale. Use rendered alpha for transparent/cutout assets rather than treating triangle hits as opacity. Candidate, not installed.
- OpenSubdiv https://opensubdiv.com/ : smooth subdivision limit surfaces; evaluate final contour rather than control cage. Preserve intentional sharp edges through crease/support loops; compare before/after at close and gameplay distance. Candidate technique, standalone library not installed.
- Blender 5.3 remeshing https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.3/modeling/meshes/retopology.html : optional voxel union on duplicate organic rock/sculpt blockouts; never mandatory, can erase narrow details and topology needed for deformation. Preserve original and compare actual renders. Blender operation not tested in this task.
## Implementation order
VF01 multi-view masks and IoU, missing/excess area, visual diff PNG; VF02 matched first-hit depth and normal maps; VF03 part proportions, projected landmarks and contour distances; VF04 curves, loft and adjustable silhouette control points; VF05 optional bevel/subdivision and localized deformation; VF06 material swatches and consistent neutral/game lighting comparisons; VF07 immutable design image, render and comparison archive; VF08 checkpoint and gallery integration.
Mask similarity is diagnostic, not artistic approval. No automatic rejection thresholds until calibrated for category. No hidden-intersection gate. Preserve intentional asymmetry, wear, gaps and silhouette changes. Compare current candidate both to approved original and design target; no blind reference mesh copying. Save masks, camera metadata, scores, source hashes and actual render evidence by revision. No extra paid API. Official documentation reviewed; new external dependencies and Blender integration unverified, licenses to check before adoption.
