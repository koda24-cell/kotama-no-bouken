# Sources and rights

Individual geometry and original small finish maps were authored for this
project by the GPT environment-art team. They are newly authored assets,
not extracted meshes, textures or symbols from Nintendo, HoYoverse or Gryphline.
The references are visual quality goals, not source assets.

Photographic PBR sources are distributed under CC0-1.0:

- [Poly Haven Mossy Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_rock), 3m surface width.
- [Poly Haven Bark Brown 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_01), 1m width.
- [Poly Haven Forrest Ground 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/forrest_ground_01), 2m width.
- [Poly Haven asset licence](https://polyhaven.com/license).

See `textures/texture-sources.json` for original download URLs, hashes,
successful and failed attempts, image sizes and conversions. The failed
ambientCG downloads were not used. Sources were resized to 512/1024 JPEG;
16-bit roughness maps were normalized before 8-bit delivery. Normals are
OpenGL +Y data, not colour textures.

Pinterest reference images are not distributed with the assets and were not
used as texture inputs. Their links and observations are recorded in the
project research document and the private reference-library handoff.
The fictional marks on the papers and stele are original patterns.

Blender Lab's GPL-3.0-or-later add-on is installed as a separate tool in the
workspace and is not bundled in these game assets.

Traceable low-saturation, damp-palette albedo derivatives are recorded in
`textures/palette-derivatives.json`; original photographed maps remain unchanged.
