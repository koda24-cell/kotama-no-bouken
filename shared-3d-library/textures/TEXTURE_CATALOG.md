# PBR texture sources

| Provider | URL | Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ambientCG | https://ambientcg.com/ | CC0 materials; check individual assets and record download hashes | catalogued only |
| Poly Haven | https://polyhaven.com/textures | CC0 textures; download at appropriate resolution with maps intact | catalogued only |

Keep BaseColor, Normal (OpenGL), Roughness, Metallic, Height, AO together. Record UV scale, resolution and whether textures are packed into GLB. Do not store an external URL as if it were a downloaded texture.
